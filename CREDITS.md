## Track & world

The circuit is an original stylized interpretation of the downtown
Indianapolis street grid (Monument Circle, Washington/Meridian/Pennsylvania
streets, Victory Field vicinity) built procedurally in code. Not affiliated
with IndyCar, the Indianapolis Motor Speedway, or the City of Indianapolis.
